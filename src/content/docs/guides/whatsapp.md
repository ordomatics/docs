---
title: Working over WhatsApp
description: What you can ask your Ordomatics instance to do.
---

Your instance answers on a WhatsApp number of its own. Anything you would
normally click through Odoo to do, you can ask for instead.

## Things to try

- *"How many of SKU-4021 are left?"*
- *"Invoice Diallo Trading for last week's delivery."*
- *"Add +221 77 000 00 00 as a new contact at Kajande."*

## Sending documents

Send a photo or PDF of an invoice, receipt or delivery note. It is read, matched
to a supplier, and turned into a draft record for you to confirm.

:::caution
Confirm drafts before posting. Document understanding is good, but an unusual
layout can still put a number in the wrong field.
:::
