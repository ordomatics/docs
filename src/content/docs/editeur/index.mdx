The Smartacus editor helps you produce administrative documents: a letter to
the town hall, a contract, an invoice or an official report. You can dictate,
have your text read aloud, ask Copilot to write or correct, and start from a
PDF or a scanned document.

The editor is online at [odoo.smartacus.pro/editor](https://odoo.smartacus.pro/editor).

<LinkButton href="https://odoo.smartacus.pro/editor" icon="external">Open the editor</LinkButton>

:::note[The editor speaks French]
The editor's interface is in French, and so are the videos. Each step below
gives the French label of the button to click, with its meaning in English
— for example **Modifier** (Edit).
:::

## What's inside

0. [Your account](/editeur/compte/): sign up, log in, top up your credits
1. [Getting to know the editor](/editeur/decouvrir/): the screen areas,
   folders and documents
2. [Writing and proofreading by voice](/editeur/voix/): dictate and have a
   document read aloud
3. [Rewriting with AI](/editeur/ia/): Régénérer, Copilot, and voice
   messages in French or Wolof
4. [Importing and extracting a document](/editeur/importer/): PDF, scan or
   photo
5. [Preview and export](/editeur/apercu/): Original and PDF views, export to
   PDF, image or Word
6. [Complete scenario](/editeur/scenario/): a scanned notice updated from
   start to finish
7. [From paper to WhatsApp](/editeur/telephone/): photograph a document
   with your phone, correct it, send it back to the phone and share it

Bonus: [become an affiliate](/editeur/affiliation/) and earn a commission on
the top-ups of the customers you refer.

:::tip[Recommended browser]
Use **Google Chrome**: dictation and reading aloud rely on the browser's
built-in voice features.
:::
