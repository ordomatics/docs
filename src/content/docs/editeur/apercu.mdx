## 5.1 Switch to preview

Click **Aperçu** (Preview — the eye) in the toolbar: the document is shown as
it will be printed, page by page. Click **Modifier** (Edit — the pencil) to go
back to writing.

<Clip src="en/5-1-apercu.mp4" caption="From writing to preview, and back" />

The standard preview is **free**. The **Aperçu IA** (AI preview) option (in
the [settings](/editeur/compte/#04-settings)) asks artificial intelligence to
rebuild the document's layout, as close as possible to the original for an
imported document; it is charged per page for each new preview (reopening
an unchanged preview costs nothing).

## 5.2 The two views: Original and PDF

For an imported document (PDF, scan or photo), the status bar offers two views
in preview mode:

- **PDF**: your version, with your changes;
- **Original**: the document as you imported it.

<Clip src="en/5-2-original-et-pdf.mp4" caption="Comparing the extraction of a photo with the original" />

Switch between them to check the extraction line by line, especially amounts
and references.

## 5.3 Export

The **Exporter** (Export) button in the ribbon offers the formats that suit
the current mode:

| Mode | Formats | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| **Aperçu** (Preview) | **PDF (.pdf)** and **Image (.png)**, identical to the preview; **Envoyer sur mon téléphone** (Send to my phone) | free |
| **Modifier** (Edit) | **Word (.docx)**, to continue in a word processor | a small fixed amount |

<Clip src="en/5-3-exporter.mp4" caption="Exporting a PDF from the preview, then the Word option in edit mode" />

A greyed-out format isn't available in the current mode: hover over it to see
which mode to switch to.

![The Exporter menu in preview mode: PDF and Image available, Word greyed out](../../../assets/editeur/5-3-exporter-apercu.png)

![The Exporter menu in edit mode: only Word is available](../../../assets/editeur/5-3-exporter-modifier.png)

### Sending to your phone

To read or pass on the document from your phone, switch to **Aperçu**
mode, open **Exporter** and choose **Envoyer sur mon téléphone**:

1. A QR code appears.
2. Scan it with the phone's camera: a page shows the document's name.
3. Tap **Télécharger** (Download), or **Partager…** (Share) to send it by
   WhatsApp, e-mail or to another service.

A document created in the editor goes as a **PDF**; an imported scan or PDF
goes as it is. No login is needed on the phone.

:::caution[A temporary code]
The code is valid for **15 minutes** and allows at most **5 downloads**.
During that time, anyone who sees the code can download the document: don't
show it in front of other people.
:::

:::note[Imported documents]
In preview mode, **Exporter** directly downloads the PDF version of your
document (or the original file if no preview has been generated yet). For a
Word export, switch back to **Modifier** mode.
:::
