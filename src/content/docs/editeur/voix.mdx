Dictation and reading aloud use the browser's voice features: they are
**free** and use no credits. They work best in **Google Chrome**. Both work in
**French**, the language of the editor.

## 2.1 Dictate

1. Click in the document where the text should appear.
2. Click **Dicter** (Dictate — the microphone) in the toolbar, then speak
   normally.
3. The text is written as you speak, at the cursor position.
4. After a few seconds of silence, dictation stops on its own. You can also
   click the microphone again to stop it.

<Clip src="en/2-1-dicter.mp4" caption="Dictating a sentence at the end of a paragraph" />

To **replace** a passage, select it before dictating: the dictated text takes
its place.

:::tip[Proofread after dictating]
Speech recognition doesn't always add capitals and final punctuation. Fix
them with the keyboard, or select the sentence and use **Régénérer**
(part 3) to rewrite it cleanly.
:::

:::note[The microphone doesn't respond?]
- The first time, the browser asks for permission to use the microphone:
  accept it.
- If you refused it, the editor shows « Accès au microphone refusé »
  (Microphone access denied): allow the microphone for this site in the
  browser settings (the icon to the left of the address), then reload the
  page.
- Outside Chrome, a message tells you that speech recognition isn't
  supported.
:::

## 2.2 Read aloud

1. To read **a passage**, select it. To read **from a given point**, simply
   click there.
2. Click **Lire à voix haute** (Read aloud — the speaker).
3. Each word is highlighted as it is read.
4. Reading stops at the end of the selection (or of the document). Click the
   speaker again to stop it earlier.

<Clip src="en/2-2-lire-a-voix-haute.mp4" caption="Reading a selected paragraph (video without sound)" />

Once reading is finished or stopped, your selection or cursor position is
restored: another click reads the same passage again.

It's a good way to **proofread** a letter before sending it, or to let
someone who struggles to read French listen to a document.
