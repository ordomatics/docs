Do you know people, businesses or public services that would benefit from the
editor? Become an **affiliate**: you earn a commission on every credit top-up
by the customers you refer, paid into your **Wave** account.

## B.1 Become an affiliate

1. Open **Mon compte**: the **Affiliation** card offers **Vendre ce
   logiciel** (Sell this software).
2. Explain your approach in a few words (who you plan to help, and how).
3. Enter the **Wave number** that will receive your commissions, if it isn't
   saved yet.
4. Click **Envoyer** (Send): your request becomes **En attente** (Pending).

<Clip src="en/bonus-1-devenir-affilie.mp4" caption="Sending an affiliation request" />

The Smartacus team reviews your request and lets you know by e-mail as soon as
it is approved.

## B.2 Refer people with your link

Once you're approved, **Mon compte** shows your **Solde parrainage** (referral
balance). Click **Voir** (View) to open your affiliate area, where your
**referral link** is. Click **Copier** (Copy), then share it (WhatsApp,
e-mail, social media…).

People who create their account **by opening this link** become your
referrals. **Every time** one of them tops up credits, you receive a
percentage of the amount.

:::tip[Link first]
The referral is recorded when the account is created: ask the people you
refer to open your link **before** signing up, otherwise the account won't be
linked to you.
:::

## B.3 Withdraw your commissions via Wave

A commission stays **pending** for a few days, while the top-up is validated,
then becomes **payable** and is added to your **payable balance**.

1. In your affiliate area, check your Wave number (the **Modifier** button to
   change it, then **Enregistrer** — Save).
2. Click **Retirer via Wave** (Withdraw via Wave) and enter the amount.
3. Click **Confirmer le retrait** (Confirm withdrawal): the amount is sent to
   your Wave account.

<Clip src="en/bonus-2-espace-affilie.mp4" caption="The affiliate area: referral link, balance and withdrawal (withdrawal cancelled in the demo)" />

A **minimum balance** is required to withdraw: until it is reached, the
affiliate area tells you. Your latest withdrawals are shown with their status:
**En cours** (In progress), **Payé** (Paid) or **Échoué** (Failed). A failed
withdrawal isn't lost: the amount stays in your balance.
