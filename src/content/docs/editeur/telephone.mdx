You have a paper document (a quote, an invoice, a letter) and your phone.
With no scanner and no cable, you bring it into the editor, correct it, then
send it back to your phone to share it.

<Clip src="en/7-du-papier-a-whatsapp.mp4" voice caption="From paper to WhatsApp, end to end (French voice-over; waits sped up): play it with sound on" />

:::note[What is illustrated in the video]
The phone is shown as a frame on the right of the screen. Two screens belong
to the phone's own system, not to the editor: **the camera** and **the share
menu**. They carry the label "Illustration"; on your phone, your own ones
open instead.
:::

## 7.1 Bring the paper in

1. On the computer, click **Importer** (Import), then **Avec mon
   téléphone** (With my phone): a QR code appears.
2. Scan it with your phone's camera. The **Photographier un document**
   (Photograph a document) page opens.
3. Tap **Photographier une page** (Photograph a page), lay the document
   flat, well lit, and capture all of it. You can also **choisir dans la
   galerie** (choose from the gallery), and add several pages.
4. Tap **Envoyer** (Send): the page opens in the editor, on the computer.

## 7.2 Extract the text and correct it

1. Click **Modifier** (Edit). The editor shows the page count and the
   price: click **Extraire** (Extract).
2. If the document carries a stamp, a signature or a logo, the editor offers
   to **keep them as images**, for an extra charge. In the video, we answer
   **Non merci** (No thanks): they are then replaced by a note in square
   brackets.
3. The text is editable, table included. In the video, the quote's validity
   goes from 30 to 15 days.

:::tip[Check the figures]
The AI can get an amount or a name wrong: check them against the paper
before sending the document.
:::

## 7.3 Send the document back to the phone

1. Switch to **Aperçu** (Preview), then click **Exporter** (Export).
2. Choose **Envoyer sur mon téléphone** (Send to my phone): a new QR code
   appears, valid for 15 minutes.
3. Scan it with your phone: the **Votre document** (Your document) page
   opens.
4. Tap **Télécharger** (Download) to keep the PDF, or **Partager…**
   (Share…) to send it right away via **WhatsApp**, e-mail or another app.

![The document sent back to the phone, ready to be shared](../../../assets/editeur/7-du-papier-a-whatsapp.png)

The **Partager…** button appears on phones whose browser can share files.
If it doesn't, download the document, then share it from your files.
