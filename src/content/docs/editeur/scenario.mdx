The president of the Médina parents' association needs to send the notice of
the back-to-school general meeting. All he has is last year's paper version,
which he scanned. In a few minutes, he updates it and exports it.

<Clip src="en/6-scenario-complet.mp4" caption="The complete scenario (waits sped up)" />

## The steps

1. **Log in** from **Mon compte**
   ([part 0](/editeur/compte/#02-log-in-and-log-out)).
2. **Import** the scanned letter, then click **Modifier** (Edit) to extract
   its text ([part 4](/editeur/importer/)).
3. **Dictate** an addition at the end of a paragraph: *« Une collation sera
   offerte à l'issue de l'assemblée. »* (Refreshments will be served after the
   meeting.) ([part 2](/editeur/voix/#21-dictate)).
4. **Ask Copilot** to change the date: *« Remplace la date de l'assemblée
   par le samedi 10 octobre 2026 à 9 heures. »* (Change the meeting date to
   Saturday 10 October 2026 at 9 am.) ([part 3](/editeur/ia/)).
5. **Listen** to the edited paragraph to proofread it
   ([part 2](/editeur/voix/#22-read-aloud)).
6. **Switch to preview**, then **export** the PDF
   ([part 5](/editeur/apercu/)).
