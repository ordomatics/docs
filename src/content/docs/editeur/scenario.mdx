the back-to-school general meeting. All he has is last year's paper version,
which he scanned. In a few minutes, he updates it and exports it.

## The steps

1. **Log in** from **Mon compte**
   ([part 0](/editeur/compte/#02-log-in-and-log-out)).
2. **Import** the scanned letter and **look through it** as it is, scrolling
   through its pages, then click **Modifier** (Edit) to extract its text
   ([part 4](/editeur/importer/)).
3. **Dictate** an addition at the end of a paragraph: *« Une collation sera
   offerte à l'issue de l'assemblée. »* (Refreshments will be served after the
   meeting.) ([part 2](/editeur/voix/#21-dictate)).
4. **Correct the dictation with Régénérer** (Regenerate): select the dictated
   paragraph, then click **Régénérer**. The capitals and punctuation that speech
   recognition leaves out are restored
   ([part 3](/editeur/ia/#31-regenerate-a-passage)).
5. **Ask Copilot** to change the date: *« Remplace la date de l'assemblée
   par le samedi 10 octobre 2026 à 9 heures. »* (Change the meeting date to
   Saturday 10 October 2026 at 9 am.) ([part 3](/editeur/ia/)).
6. **Listen** to the edited paragraph to proofread it
   ([part 2](/editeur/voix/#22-read-aloud)).
7. **Switch to preview**, then **export** the PDF
   ([part 5](/editeur/apercu/)).

:::tip[More room for the document]
When the document is shown as a PDF (right after importing, or in preview),
collapse **Copilot** and **Mes fichiers** with the buttons at the top right:
the page then takes the full width. Reopen Copilot when you need it.
:::
