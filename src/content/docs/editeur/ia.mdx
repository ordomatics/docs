Two artificial intelligence tools help you write: **Régénérer** (Regenerate)
to correct a specific passage, and **Copilot** to edit the document following
your instructions, written or spoken. Copilot writes in French, the language
of the editor's documents.

## 3.1 Regenerate a passage

**Régénérer** corrects and improves the selected passage: spelling, capitals,
punctuation, wording. It's ideal right after dictating.

1. Select the passage to improve.
2. Click **Régénérer** (the two arrows in a circle).
3. The new text is written in place of the old one.

<Clip src="en/3-1-regenerer.mp4" caption="A dictated sentence, corrected by Régénérer" />

Not happy with the result? **Annuler** (Undo, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>)
takes you back to the original text.

💳 Charged by the number of words produced. With no selection, a message asks
you to choose the text to regenerate first.

## 3.2 Edit the document with Copilot

The **Copilot** panel, on the right, works on the open document. Describe what
you want: Copilot writes, then **edits the document directly**.

<Clip src="en/3-2-copilot.mp4" caption="Adding a paragraph requested from Copilot" />

A few example requests (in French, like the documents):

- **Personal paperwork**: *« Rédige une lettre au bailleur pour signaler une
  fuite d'eau dans la cuisine depuis le 2 septembre. »* (Write a letter to the
  landlord reporting a water leak in the kitchen since 2 September.)
- **Business**: *« Transforme ce devis en facture n° 2026-045, payable à
  30 jours. »* (Turn this quote into invoice no. 2026-045, payable in 30
  days.)
- **Public administration**: *« Mets ce document au format d'une
  convocation, avec l'en-tête de la République du Sénégal. »* (Format this
  document as an official notice, with the Republic of Senegal header.)

:::tip[Give it the facts]
Copilot doesn't invent amounts, numbers or clauses: give it the exact
information. When something is missing, it inserts a **[à compléter]** (to be
completed) field and tells you.
:::

Every change made by Copilot can be undone with **Annuler**, like a change
made with the keyboard.

💳 Only changes to your document are charged: a small fixed amount per change,
plus the number of words Copilot writes into it. Its answers in the chat are
free.

## 3.3 Talk to Copilot, in French or Wolof

### A voice message

Click the **microphone** in the Copilot panel, speak, then click again to stop
and **send**. You can speak **French or Wolof**: the message is transcribed,
translated into French if needed, and Copilot applies it to the document.

<Clip src="en/3-3-message-vocal.mp4" caption="A request in Wolof: « Soppi date bi ci vingt septembre » (change the date to 20 September)" />

The transcription appears in your message: check it at a glance.

### An existing recording

The **Joindre un fichier** (Attach a file — the paperclip) button also accepts
audio recordings, for example a hearing recorded on a phone. Attach it, then
give your instruction.

### Case study: from a hearing to an official report

At the Thiaroye gendarmerie station, two shopkeepers come to file a complaint
after a burglary at their shops. They speak Wolof; the gendarme records the
hearing on his phone.

1. He creates an empty document, for example *« PV d'audition - vol au
   marché »* (Hearing report - market theft).
2. He attaches the recording to Copilot.
3. He types his instruction: *« Rédige le procès-verbal d'audition des deux
   plaignants à partir de cet enregistrement. »* (Write the hearing report
   of the two complainants from this recording.)
4. Copilot transcribes the hearing, translates it into French, then writes the
   report: the complainants' identities, each one's statement, what the guard
   saw, signatures. Information missing from the recording (date, place,
   the guard's name, the officer's rank) is left **[à compléter]**.

<Clip src="en/3-4-audition-proces-verbal.mp4" caption="A Wolof recording turned into an official report" />

:::caution[Always proofread the report]
Copilot sticks to what was stated, but the transcription and the write-up can
still contain discrepancies. Check names, amounts, dates and who said what,
listening to the recording again if needed, before having it signed.
:::

The gendarme then completes the document with the keyboard or by dictation
(part 2), and exports it (part 5).

💳 The transcription is charged by the number of words transcribed, on top of
the words Copilot writes into the document.
