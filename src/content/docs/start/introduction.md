Ordomatics runs a full **Odoo 18** ERP for your business — inventory, accounting,
CRM, HR and purchasing — and lets you operate it from WhatsApp rather than from a
form-filling interface.

## Why it works this way

Most small and mid-sized businesses never adopt an ERP because the setup cost is
measured in consultants and months. If the system answers a WhatsApp message
instead, that barrier is gone.

The conversation is the interface. Odoo is still underneath, in full, and always
available in a browser when you want it.

## What you get

- Your own Odoo 18 instance, database and file storage
- A WhatsApp number your team and customers can talk to
- Document understanding — photograph an invoice, get a posted bill
- Your choice of AI model, including one running privately
