## 1. Send a message

Message us on [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/221773649575). That is the sign-up form —
there is nothing to fill in.

We will ask for your business name, the country you operate in, and what you want
to run first (stock, invoicing, customers, or all of it).

## 2. We provision

Your Odoo 18 instance, database and object storage are created automatically. You
get a URL of your own and an administrator login.

How isolated that instance is depends on your plan — see [Plans](/reference/plans/).

## 3. Start working

Ask the system questions, raise invoices, track stock. Your data is yours from the
first message.

:::note
Migrating from an existing Odoo? It is the same Odoo underneath, so your current
database can usually be brought across. Tell us your version and module list.
:::
