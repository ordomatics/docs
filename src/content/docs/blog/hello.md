This documentation site is new, and deliberately small. Rather than publish the
internal runbooks we already had — which describe our own infrastructure, not your
deployment — we started from what a customer actually needs to know.

Right now that is: what Ordomatics is, how to get an instance, how the plans differ,
and what you can ask over WhatsApp. Guides will grow from real questions as they
come in.

It is bilingual from day one: every page has an English and a French version, and
the language switcher is in the header.

If something you need is missing, tell us on
[WhatsApp](https://wa.me/221773649575) and it will likely become the next page.
